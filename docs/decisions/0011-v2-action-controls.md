# ADR 0011: Primary-objective action roles

Status: accepted.

Transaction tax and collusion investigation are substantive primary-welfare remedies. Price ceiling and supply subsidy are intentional negative controls under the transfer-correct primary ledger: they are plausible-sounding interventions but were structurally dominated throughout the released calibration, including a local subsidy-rate sweep. Do-nothing is an abstention control. All five remain selectable and are re-evaluated under every alternative objective; the labels do not enter prompts or scoring.
